# English-to-German Learning

This context describes the learning experience for English-speaking adults living and working in Germany.

## Learning content

**CEFR level**:
A1, A2, or B1 proficiency grouping used to organize lessons.
_Avoid_: Beginner/intermediate labels when a specific CEFR level is intended

**Situation**:
A recognizable daily-life or office-work context that gives a German phrase or language pattern a practical purpose.
_Avoid_: Scenario-free phrase list

**Quiz**:
A short exercise that asks the learner to understand or produce useful German in a situation and gives explanatory feedback.
_Avoid_: Test, when referring to ordinary practice

**Eselsbrücke**:
A memorable cue connected to a real situation or language pattern, with an explanation of where the cue is useful and where it stops applying.
_Avoid_: Grammar rule, mnemonic rule

## Learner progress

**Learner progress**:
Lesson completion and scores associated with one learner's browser, without an account or cross-device synchronization.
_Avoid_: Profile, account progress
